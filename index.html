    Quick answers

    Still unsure? Just message us.

    How does booking work?

    Choose your basket, day and estate above and tap Send on WhatsApp. We reply to confirm your slot. You can also just DM us on Instagram or WhatsApp.

    Which basket should I choose?

    Basic basket is the tall lidded basket and Big basket is the wide round one with handles. You'll see both pictured in the pricing section. Basic suits one regular load, Big basket a larger pile. If you're not sure, pick one, or send us a photo of your laundry on WhatsApp and we'll tell you which fits.

    Is ironing included?

    No. Ironing is optional and priced separately: Basic KSh 500 or A lot KSh 900. Add it when you want crisp clothes, skip it when you don't.

    Where do you work?

    We serve homes along Thika Road, Nairobi. Enter your estate when you book and we'll confirm we can reach you.

    When are you available?

    Monday to Friday 11 AM to 2 PM, Saturday 9 AM to 4 PM and Sunday 8 AM to 3 PM.
